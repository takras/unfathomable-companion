## Game material in this repository and app

Not covered by this project's MIT license and not licensed for reuse:

- `src/content/rules-reference.json` and `src/content/rules-abyss.ts` - rules text from the
  Unfathomable Rules Reference and the From the Abyss rulebook.
- `src/data/preludes.ts` - prelude card text.
- `assets/portraits/`, `src/assets/portraits/` - character portraits cut from the character cards.
- `assets/preludes/`, `src/assets/preludes/` - prelude card images.
- `src/assets/icons/` - activation, track, resource and skill icons from the cards and reference sheets.
- Game, character and card names, and the succession lists in `src/data/`.

The original scans and rulebook PDFs (`source/`) are kept out of the repository.

If you are a rights holder and want any of this material changed or removed, please open an issue.
