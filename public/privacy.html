Privacy Policy

Unfathomable Companion · Effective 20 November 2026

Unfathomable Companion is an unofficial, fan-made helper for the board game Unfathomable, available as a website at unfathomable.takras.net and as apps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

To keep your game when you close the app or reload the page, Unfathomable Companion saves your current game on your own device: the expansion and options you chose, the characters in play, who holds the Captain and Keeper of the Tome titles, who is in the Brig or revealed, the monster activations you picked, your prelude settings and the preludes drawn, and the game log, plus your choice of paper colour and language. This information:

  • stays on your device (the browser's local storage, or the app's private storage on Android and iOS);
  • is never sent to us or to anyone else;
  • contains no names, contact details or other personal information, only game characters and options.

You can delete it at any time with the New game button in the app, by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No network requests of its own: all content, images and fonts are included in the app.

The Android app declares the standard Internet permission that its built-in web view uses, but the app itself does not send or receive data. Links to other websites (for example in the credits) open only when you tap them; those sites have their own privacy policies.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address, as it does for any website, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Unfathomable Companion itself does not receive this information.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
